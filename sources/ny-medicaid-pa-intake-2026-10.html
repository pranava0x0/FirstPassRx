Prior Authorization Resources

To obtain a prior authorization call (877) 309-9493 or fax the appropriate Prior Authorization form to (800) 268-2990. The clinical call center is available 24 hours a day, 7 days per week.

Electronic prior authorizations for NYRx, the Medicaid Pharmacy Program can also be obtained via CoverMyMeds®.

PAXpress is a web-based application available for initiating prior authorization requests.
