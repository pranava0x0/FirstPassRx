Amvuttra

  • Documentation of all of the following is required for polyneuropathy of hereditary transthyretin-mediated (hATTR) amyloidosis:
    • appropriate diagnosis; and
    • member is ≥ 18 years of age; and
    • prescriber is a rheumatologist or neurologist or consult notes from a rheumatologist or neurologist are provided; and
    • baseline polyneuropathy disability (PND) score of I, II, IIIa, or IIIb; and
    • appropriate dosing.
  • Documentation of all of the following is required for cardiomyopathy of wild-type transthyretin-mediated (ATTRwt) or hATTR amyloidosis:
    • appropriate diagnosis; and
    • member is ≥ 18 years of age; and
    • prescriber is a cardiologist or consult notes from a cardiologist are provided; and
    • appropriate dosing; and
    • requested agent will not be used in combination with Attruby, Vyndamax, or Vyndaqel; and
    • one of the following: 
      • results from genetic testing confirming mutations in the TTR gene; or
      • presence of amyloid deposits in biopsy tissue with confirmed TTR; or
      • TTR precursor protein identification by scintigraphy, immunohistochemistry, or mass spectrometry; and
    • one of the following: 
      • inadequate response or adverse reaction to one or contraindication to both: Attruby, Vyndamax or Vyndaqel; or 
      • NYHA Class III heart failure; or
      • concurrent diagnosis of hATTR-PN.

 

Attruby

  • Documentation of all of the following is required for cardiomyopathy of wild-type transthyretin-mediated (ATTRwt) or hereditary transthyretin-mediated (hATTR) amyloidosis:
    • appropriate diagnosis; and
    • member is ≥ 18 years of age; and
    • prescriber is a cardiologist or consult notes from a cardiologist are provided; and
    • requested agent will not be used in combination with Amvuttra, Vyndamax, or Vyndaqel; and
    • one of the following:
      • results from genetic testing confirming mutations in the TTR gene; or
      • presence of amyloid deposits in biopsy tissue with confirmed TTR; or
      • TTR precursor protein identification by immunohistochemistry, scintigraphy, or mass spectrometry; and

Filsuvez

  • Documentation of all of the following is required:
    • diagnosis of dystrophic or junctional epidermolysis bullosa (DEB or JEB); and
    • member is ≥ six months of age; and
    • prescriber is a specialist (e.g., dermatologist, geneticist, histopathologist) or consult notes from a specialist are provided; and
    • copy of a genetic test confirming diagnosis of DEB or JEB (e.g., mutation of COL7A1 gene or PLOD3 gene for DEB or mutation of LAMA3, LAMB3, LAMC2, COL17A1, ITGA3, ITGA6, or ITGB4 genes for JEB); and
    • member has at least one partial thickness wound that is clean in appearance and does not appear infected; and
    • for the diagnosis of DEB, requested agent will not be used in combination with Vyjuvek or Zevaskyn on the same target wounds.
  • For recertification, documentation of all of the following is required:
    • requested agent is not being applied on target wounds that have completely healed; and
    • positive response to therapy as indicated by one of the following: 
      • decrease in wound size; or
      • decrease in pain or itch severity for target wound sites associated with dressing changes.

Nexobrid

  • Documentation of all of the following is required:
    • diagnosis of deep partial thickness and/or full thickness thermal burns; and
    • prescriber is a specialist (e.g., dermatologist, burn specialist) or consult notes from a specialist are provided; and
    • for members ≥ 18 years of age, one of the following:
      • requested quantity is one unit; or
      • requested quantity is two units and BSA of wound area is > 15% and ≤ 20%; or 
    • for members ≥ six and < 18 years of age, requested quantity is one unit and BSA of wound area is ≤ 15%; or
    • for members < six years of age, requested quantity is one unit and BSA of wound area is ≤ 10%.

Santyl

  • Documentation of all of the following is required:
    • diagnosis of chronic dermal ulcers or severely burned areas; and
    • number and size of the ulcers and/or size of lesion intended for treatment; and
    • one of the following:
      • ineligibility for surgical intervention alone; or
      • ineligibility for autolytic debridement; or
      • the requested agent is being used in combination with surgery and/or debridement; and
    • requested duration of treatment.
  • For recertification, documentation of response to the requested agent and ulcer has decreased in size is required.

Vyjuvek

  • Documentation of all of the following is required:
    • diagnosis of DEB; and
    • copy of a genetic test confirming diagnosis of dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa (e.g., mutation of COL7A1 gene); and
    • prescriber is a specialist (i.e., dermatologist, geneticist, histopathologist, etc.) or consult notes from a specialist are provided; and
    • member has at least one cutaneous wound that is clean in appearance with adequate granulation tissue, has excellent vascularization, and does not appear infected; and
    • for a diagnosis of DEB, requested agent will not be used in combination with Filsuvez or Zevaskyn on the same target wounds; and
    • appropriate dosing.
  • For recertification, documentation of all of the following is required:
    • one of the following:
      • complete wound healing of at least one wound after six months of treatment; or
      • clinical rationale for continued treatment despite lack of efficacy; and
    • member has at least one cutaneous wound that is clean in appearance with adequate granulation tissue, has excellent vascularization, and does not appear infected.

 Zevaskyn

  • Documentation of all of the following is required:
    • diagnosis of recessive dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa (RDEB); and
    • medical records or copy of a genetic test confirming diagnosis of RDEB (e.g., biallelic mutations of COL7A1 gene); and
    • prescriber is a specialist (i.e., dermatologist) in the treatment of RDEB or consult notes from a specialist are provided; and
    • at least one wound site requiring treatment with all of the following:
      • wound area must be ≥ 20 cm2; and
      • wound has been open for ≥ six months; and
      • wound is classified as Stage 2, defined as partial thickness loss of dermis presenting as a shallow open ulcer with a pink or red wound bed, without slough or bruising; and
      • wound is free of infection; and
    • total number and size of wound site(s) intended for treatment; and
    • anticipated number of sheets required for treatment of wound site(s); and
    • inadequate response, adverse reaction, or contraindication to Vyjuvek; and
    • the requested agent will not be used in combination with Vyjuvek or Filsuvez on the same target wounds; and
    • treatment procedure will be performed at a qualified treatment center; and
    • one of the following:
      • the requested agent will not be used on wound(s) that are currently healed or have been previously treated with Zevaskyn; or
      • clinical rationale for retreatment of Zevaskyn on the same wound area; and
    • one of the following:
      • member is ≥ six years of age; or
      • both of the following:
        • member is ≥ six months to < six years of age; and
        • ability to remain immobile and keep surgical area undisturbed for five to ten days post transplantation.

III.  Evaluation Criteria for Approval

Please note: In the case where the prior authorization (PA) status column indicates PA, both the brand and generic (if available) require PA. Typically, the generic is preferred when available unless the brand-name drug appears on the MassHealth Brand Name Preferred Over Generic Drug List. In general, when requesting the non-preferred version, whether the brand or generic, the prescriber must provide medical records documenting an inadequate response or adverse reaction to the preferred version, in addition to satisfying the criteria for the drug itself.

  • All prior-authorization requests must include clinical diagnosis, drug name, dose, and frequency.
  • Dispensing in a 90-day supply of medication may be mandated or allowable for agents in this therapeutic class (designated by M90 or A90, respectively, in the Drug Notes section above). Applicable quantity limits are described below as units per day, per month, per 28 days, or as clinically appropriate, and may be extrapolated for fills of longer day supply. 
  • A preferred drug may be designated for this therapeutic class. In general, MassHealth requires a trial of the preferred drug or clinical rationale for prescribing a non-preferred drug within a therapeutic class. Additional information about these agents, including PA requirements and preferred products, can be found within the MassHealth Drug List at www.mass.gov/druglist.
  • For recertification requests, approval may require submission of additional documentation including, but not limited to, documentation of: some or all criteria for the original approval; response to therapy; clinical rationale for continuation of use; status of member’s condition; appropriate diagnosis; appropriate age; appropriate dose, frequency, and duration of use for requested medication; complete treatment plan; current laboratory values; and member’s current weight.
  • Additional criteria may apply depending upon diagnosis and/or requested medication (see below). Other factors, including rebate and FDA-approval status, may change independently of scheduled MassHealth updates, which may result in additional restrictions.
