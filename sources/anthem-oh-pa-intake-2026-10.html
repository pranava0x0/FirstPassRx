    Contact us

    IMPORTANT NOTICE: If your medical and pharmacy benefits are covered through Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield, or Anthem Blue Cross, please call the number (Member Services or Pharmacy Services) on the back of your member ID card, or visit your health plan’s website for help.

    Members

    For help, please call the Pharmacy Services number on the back of your member ID card to speak with a CarelonRx representative. (Or, if no Pharmacy Services number, please call Member Services to address your needs.)

    To manage your prescriptions, order refills, set up home delivery, and check medication pricing, visit manage your prescriptions.

    Providers

    For information about prior authorization, prescription exceptions, and utilization management, call 833-293-0659 or fax 844-521-6940.

    For information about home delivery, call 833-396-0309 or fax 833-389-4172.

    For information about specialty pharmacy and prescribing instructions, please visit our Provider page to learn more.

    Business

    Employers and plan sponsors who want to learn more about CarelonRx:
    Reach out here and you will be contacted by a member of the CarelonRx team.

    Subscribe to our monthly CarelonRx News, and we'll deliver clinical reports, industry trends, company news, and PBM insights to your Inbox.

    General business inquiries can be sent to:
    CarelonRx
    220 Virginia Avenue
    Indianapolis, IN 46204

    Pharmacy network inquiries: To inquire about joining the CarelonRx pharmacy network, please email RxServices@cvshealth.com. Inquiries are facilitated by a dedicated team of analysts via our partners at CVS. A network enrollment analyst will respond within three business days.
