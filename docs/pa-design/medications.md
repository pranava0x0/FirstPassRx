# What to cover next
Decision note · 11 November 2026

**Use two queues.** To reduce PA work, start with Washington ophthalmic cyclosporine and deepen the existing incretin guides. To improve everyday lookup, pilot latanoprost and allopurinol. An often-filled medicine can be a useful lookup even if it rarely needs a PA. Neither queue is a national ranking of PA volume.

| Order within lane | What to research | Demand signal | What is missing; first unit of work |
|---|---|---|---|
| PA 1 · new topic | Ophthalmic cyclosporine: Restasis/generic, Cequa, Vevye | 1,973 WA requests; fifth in Figure 17, reported by five carriers | No eye topic or Restasis entry on main. Start with one WA payer’s pharmacy formulary and ocular criteria. Separate emulsion vs solution, product and indication; the regulator grouped several products (including Verkazia), so 1,973 is **not** a dry-eye-only count. Never reuse systemic cyclosporine policy. |
| PA 2 · deepen existing | Semaglutide (Ozempic, Rybelsus, Wegovy); tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound) | 18,505 and 8,119 WA requests, respectively; first and second | Both already appear in WA weight and diabetes guides. In the existing WA barrier lists, 6 semaglutide and 5 tirzepatide entries matched by name; none had attached criteria. Pick one WA plan/product with published criteria; distinguish indication, formulation, initiation vs renewal, and explicit benefit exclusion. Do not build another GLP-1 topic. |
| PA 3 · deepen existing | Lisdexamfetamine / Vyvanse | 3,266 WA requests; third | WA ADHD already has 3 named barrier entries, none with attached criteria. Verify one plan’s exact brand/generic and formulation rules. Do not infer a step from the existence of a preferred product. |
| PA 4 · extend existing backlog | Dupilumab / Dupixent, respiratory indication | 1,886 WA requests across indications | WA dermatology already contains preferred entries and two barrier entries with criteria. The severe-asthma biologics backlog already names dupilumab. Validate a respiratory-specific pharmacy policy and benefit channel before extending it; the WA number cannot tell us how many requests were for asthma. |
| Volume 1 · new topic | Glaucoma: latanoprost drops | 10,260,351 estimated US prescriptions in 2024, rank 70 | No latanoprost record. During the ocular source read, evaluate generic drops, exact product/form and preservative-free products separately. A separate glaucoma guide; never present these as dry-eye alternatives. |
| Volume 2 · new topic | Gout maintenance: allopurinol; investigate febuxostat in the same payer document | 14,851,781 estimated US allopurinol prescriptions in 2024 | No allopurinol entry. Pilot one state’s maintenance therapy lane; keep acute flare treatment separate. Its higher fill volume does not establish higher PA burden. Schedule after eye care because it cannot reuse the ocular policy read. |

**Evidence.** PA counts are from [Washington OIC, Figure 17, plan year 2024](https://www.insurance.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-03/2026-prior-authorization-report_0.pdf#page=24). Carriers submitted their own top-ten codes; the state aggregated those submissions. This is neither all US payers nor a count of patients or denials. Prescription figures are ClinCalc’s MEPS-based national estimates for [latanoprost](https://clincalc.com/DrugStats/Drugs/Latanoprost) and [allopurinol](https://clincalc.com/Drugstats/Drugs/Allopurinol); they count prescriptions, not unique people or PA requests. No comparable national drug-level PA count was verified.

**Gap check.** Compared the canonical guides on main (5ac5115) with PR #33 (de19362). For the WA counts, matched brand/generic names in paRequired[].drug and checked whether criteria was populated; preferred and alternative entries were inspected separately. Repeated entries across plan/class combinations are intentional. Counts measure data depth, not demand. Other top-ten ingredients (e.g., adalimumab and rimegepant) already appear in the corpus. PPIs, thyroid agents, oral steroids, antibiotics, BPH drugs and loop diuretics already have a six-topic rollout; continue that queue separately.

**Definition of done per pilot:** one named state, payer product, drug, formulation and indication; link the dated formulary row and any distinct PA criteria/form; distinguish preferred, PA, step, quantity limit, explicit exclusion, and not found. Publish a coverage-only result if criteria cannot be verified. Do not turn this planning list into patient-specific prescribing advice or bulk-add it across plans.
